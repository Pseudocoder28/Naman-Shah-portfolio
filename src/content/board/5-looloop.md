Students say what they want, answer a short questionnaire or browse everything. Events from Ticketmaster, the University of Waterloo and WUSA are filtered by interests, time, budget and travel distance, and signed-in students join a circle for each event through Supabase Auth. I'm the top contributor, with 21 of the project's 31 commits.
